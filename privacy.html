Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 18, 2026

Welcome to HeyVino ("we," "us," or "our"). We operate HeyVinoWine.com, a wine promotional code aggregator. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights around it. We keep things simple — because honestly, nobody has time for a 30-page legal document.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information. Here's what we may collect:

  • Usage data — pages visited, time on site, browser type, and general location (country/city level) via Google Analytics. This data is anonymized and aggregated.
  • Email address — only if you voluntarily sign up for deal alerts or contact us directly.
  • Cookies — we use standard analytics cookies (Google Analytics) to understand how visitors use the site. No advertising cookies or third-party tracking pixels beyond this.

We do not collect payment information, social security numbers, or any sensitive personal data. We do not require account creation to use HeyVino.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To understand how people use HeyVino and improve the experience
  • To send deal alerts if you've opted in (you can unsubscribe anytime)
  • To respond to inquiries sent to HeyVinoMarketing@gmail.com

We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with third parties for marketing purposes. Full stop.

3. Promo Codes & Third Party Links

HeyVino aggregates publicly available promotional codes from winery and retailer newsletters. When you click "Visit Site" or use a promo code, you are leaving HeyVino and visiting a third-party website. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before making a purchase.

HeyVino may earn a commission on purchases made through certain affiliate links at no additional cost to you.

4. Cookies

We use Google Analytics to collect anonymized data about site traffic and usage. Google Analytics uses cookies — small text files stored on your device. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

You can also disable cookies in your browser settings at any time, though this may affect some site functionality.

5. Data Retention

If you've provided your email address for deal alerts, we retain it until you unsubscribe. Analytics data is retained per Google Analytics default settings (26 months). We don't hold onto your data longer than necessary.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights including:

  • The right to access the personal data we hold about you
  • The right to request deletion of your data
  • The right to opt out of marketing communications
  • The right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, email us at HeyVinoMarketing@gmail.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

HeyVino is intended for users of legal drinking age in their respective country. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 21 years of age in the United States or under the legal drinking age in other jurisdictions.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top. Continued use of HeyVino after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
